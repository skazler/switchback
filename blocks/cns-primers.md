# CNS Primers

Before sprint / power work, after general warmup.

- Dynamic warmups (see library group `Dynamic Warmups`)
- Sprinter iso hold — 3 × 3–5s
- Weighted pogos
- Banded wall sprints — 2 × 10–15 (full activation)
- Sled first step — 2 × 5 each
