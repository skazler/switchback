# Complex Training — Legs

Heavy lift immediately followed by its plyometric "drop-in" (post-activation potentiation). Alternate Week A / Week B.

| Week | Lift (3 × 8–15, rest 2:00) | Drop in |
|---|---|---|
| A+B | Trap bar deadlift | Tuck jumps |
| A | Step ups w/ knee drive (bench height) | Step up jumps |
| A | Lateral squats (back squat loaded) | Skater hops |
| B | Lunges | Lunge jumps |
| B | LM curtsy lunge w/ knee drive | SL 180° rotational hops |
