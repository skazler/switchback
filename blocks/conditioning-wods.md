# Named Conditioning Workouts

**Template — 5 rounds:** 15 front loaded sandbag pickups · 15 goblet squats · 15 kettlebell swings · 10 devils press · 5/side rotational landings. Swap in anything from library group `Plug-and-Play`.

**Asset** — EMOM 20: 3 burpees, 6 pushups, 9 air squats, sprint length of bay
**Brute** — AMRAP 20: 200m run, 30 mountain climbers, 20 alt gorilla rows (35s)
**Combat** — 3 rounds, rest 90s: 200m sled drag (mod-heavy), 30 box step overs (20"), 20 russian KB swings (70lbs), 15 up downs, 10 pushups, 50ft bear crawl
**Crucible Reqs** — 100 each: up-downs, pushups, 4-ct mountain climbers, 2-ct lunges, 4-ct jumping jacks
**Deck of Cards**
**Dreaded** — 6 rounds: 1:00 wall squat hold, 15 pushups, 20 reverse lunges, 15 hanging knee raises, 200m run
**Drop** — 20 rounds: 3 burpees, 4 squats, 5 pushups, 6 lunges
**Endurance Circuit** — 5 rounds: 20 pushups, 20 air squats, 15 burpees, 20 lunges, 30s plank
**Finite** — 10 DB goblet squats, 10 lateral hops over DB, 10 alt DB hang clean jerks, 10 lateral hops over DB
**Fraser's EMOM** — EMOM 40: 1:00 each row / pullups·toes-to-bar / bike / cleans·DLs·d-ball slams
**Magee** — 50-40-30-20-10: up downs, pushups, 4-ct mountain climbers, 4-ct jumping jacks
**Payment** (full gear) — EMOM 40: 200m farmers carry (35/25), 10 box step ups (24"), 200m walk, 10 hand release pushups
**Primed** — EMOM 21: 20 alt gorilla rows (50/35), 20 weighted jump squats (20/15), 1 min high plank
**Prison Workout** — 5 rounds: 2:00 AMRAP each — burpees, pushups, lunges, squats, up downs
**Shadow** — 6 rounds: 250m row, 10 burpees, 10 KB swings (53lbs)
**Stretch** — 3 rounds: 800m run mod-hard + 2:00 max pushups + 2:00 max situps → 2 rounds: 600m + 1:30 each → 1 round: 400m + 1:00 each
**Total** — bike/situps ladder: 50/10, 40/20, 30/30, 20/40, 10/50
